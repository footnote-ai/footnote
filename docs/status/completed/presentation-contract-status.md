# Presentation Flow

Status: the authority-first presentation flow is implemented. Presentation drafts are optional wording suggestions; authoritative generation and the normal assessment/revision loop own the answer. Candidate admission checks prose usability only and does not assess facts, grounding, policy, safety, or persona correctness.

The former contract-drift issue [#563](https://github.com/footnote-ai/footnote/issues/563) and workflow-budget issue [#548](https://github.com/footnote-ai/footnote/issues/548) are closed. The open model-strength policy issue [#564](https://github.com/footnote-ai/footnote/issues/564) is a separate follow-up, not unfinished presentation-contract work.

See [Prompt Resolution](../../architecture/prompt-resolution.md) and [Workflow](../../architecture/workflow.md) for maintained behavior and terminology.
