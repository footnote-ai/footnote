# AI Use Disclosure

Footnote is built with AI assistance. I want to be direct about that because a
project centered on transparency should also be transparent about how it is
made.

I'm a junior developer working on Footnote in the limited free time I have
outside a full-time job. The project is more ambitious than I could reasonably
build alone in that time. AI tools help make it possible, while giving me a way
to learn and take on work beyond my current experience.

Most of my day-to-day coding work uses Codex on the $20 USD/month ChatGPT Plus
plan. I also occasionally use ChatGPT and the free version of Claude to talk
through ideas or refine a plan.

This puts me in more of a director/reviewer role. I decide what Footnote should
do, give the AI tools context and constraints, review their work, refine as
needed, and choose what gets published. I take responsibility for those choices
and for everything I push.

I also want to be honest about the limits of that review. I cannot claim to
have read and independently verified every new line of code. Generated work can
_look_ convincing while being wrong, unnecessary, or misaligned to the
project.

I've put guardrails around the process: rules for coding agents, a documentation
structure they can use for context, automated checks, and CI/CD. I try to keep
changes small and focused. Important branches receive CodeRabbit review, and I
make the final decision before anything is merged or released.

Those measures help, but problems can still slip through the cracks. I
welcome careful review and reports from people who find them.

Footnote is both a project I hope to ship and an ongoing experiment in
using AI responsibly. I expect this process to keep changing, and I will update
this disclosure as it does.

_Written by Jordan, creator and maintainer._
